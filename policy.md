# Privacy Policy for ResQher

**Effective Date:** May 26, 2026

ResQher is a women safety application designed to help users request emergency assistance, manage safety-related information, and access support features. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect user information.

By using ResQher, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

ResQher may collect the following types of information:

### Personal Information

We may collect information such as:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- User role or account type
- Emergency contact information

### Location Information

ResQher may collect location information when users use safety features such as SOS alerts, emergency reporting, or location sharing.

Location data is used only to support safety-related features.

### Authentication Information

We may use Firebase Authentication to verify users through phone number OTP or other login methods.

Firebase may process authentication-related information such as phone numbers and verification status.

### Device and Usage Information

We may collect basic technical information such as:

- Device type
- App usage activity
- Error logs
- App performance data

This helps us improve the app and fix technical issues.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Verify users through phone number authentication
- Send OTP verification codes
- Provide SOS and emergency support features
- Share emergency alerts with selected contacts or authorized responders
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized access
- Provide user support

---

## 3. Emergency Contacts and Safety Features

If a user adds emergency contacts, ResQher may store and use those contact details only for safety-related purposes.

Emergency contact information may be used to send alerts, location details, or emergency messages when the user activates safety features.

Users should only add emergency contacts with appropriate permission.

---

## 4. Location Data Usage

Location data may be used when:

- The user activates SOS or emergency features
- The user shares location for safety purposes
- The app needs location to provide emergency assistance features

We do not sell location data.

We do not use location data for advertising purposes.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

We may share limited information only in the following cases:

- With emergency contacts chosen by the user
- With authorized emergency responders or administrators, when required for safety features
- With service providers such as Firebase, used for authentication, database, hosting, or app functionality
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect the safety, rights, or security of users or others

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

ResQher may use third-party services, including Firebase services provided by Google.

These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples include:

- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Firestore or Realtime Database
- Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Firebase Storage
- Firebase Analytics, if enabled

Users are encouraged to review Google Firebase privacy and security information.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration.

However, no digital system is completely secure. Users should also protect their account credentials and device access.

---

## 8. Data Retention

We keep user information only as long as necessary to provide app services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve safety features.

Users may request deletion of their account or personal data by contacting us.

## 9. User Rights

Users may request to:

- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete their account or personal data
- Withdraw permission for certain features, such as location access
- Stop using the app at any time

Some features may not work properly if required permissions are disabled.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.
